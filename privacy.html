Privacy Policy

Product: Phishing Detector Pro | Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Zero Data Exfiltration: Phishing Detector Pro operates entirely on your local device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data or browsing activity.

1. Data Collection & Processing

  • 100% Local Threat Analysis: All heuristic computations, domain entropy calculations, brand impersonation detection, and link safety evaluations occur locally inside your browser sandbox.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): The extension never collects or logs your name, email, credentials, IP address, or browsing history.
  • No Remote Telemetry or Tracking: There are no third-party tracking scripts, external analytics, or remote telemetry servers connected to this extension.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

To provide real-time protection against malicious websites, Phishing Detector Pro requests standard browser permissions used strictly for local security processing:

  • tabs & activeTab: Used exclusively to inspect the domain of the active tab and display real-time safety scores on the extension badge.
  • scripting: Used to render real-time security badges on links hovered on web pages and display phishing warning overlays when dangerous pages are detected.
  • storage: Used to securely store user preferences, customized whitelist/blacklist domains, and local scan history within your browser's local sandbox.
  • webNavigation: Used to detect navigation events locally and perform instant heuristic analysis before credential entry.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, lease, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
  • No data ever leaves your device.
