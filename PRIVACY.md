# Privacy Policy for Phishing Detector Pro

**Effective Date:** August 27, 2026

Phishing Detector Pro ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our browser extension.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Processing
* **Local Processing:** Phishing Detector Pro performs all threat analysis, heuristic checks, domain entropy calculations, and link safety evaluations **locally on your device**.
* **No Personal Data Collected:** The Extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or track any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, IP addresses, credentials, or personal data.
* **No External Data Exfiltration:** No URLs, page content, or user inputs leave your browser.

---

## 2. Browser Permissions Usage
* **`tabs` & `activeTab`:** Used strictly to inspect the active site's domain to display real-time safety scores in the popup badge.
* **`scripting`:** Used to display safety badges on hovered links and show safety warning overlays when phishing threats are detected.
* **`storage`:** Used to save your local settings, trusted domain whitelists, and threat scan history directly within your browser's local storage.
* **`webNavigation`:** Used to monitor page navigation events locally for instant domain threat detection.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* We do **not** share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.
* No third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or external telemetry are used in this Extension.

---

## 4. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this repository.
